Privacy Policy

Learn French Offline: Ascend

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This app ("Learn French Offline: Ascend", the "App") is published by Balanced (the "Developer", "we", "us"). This policy explains, in plain language, what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to collect as little as possible: the App is built to work fully offline, with no account required.

The short version. Your learning lives on your phone. You do not need an account to use the App. If you choose to sign in with Google, we store what is needed for the optional online leaderboard and a backup of your progress, so it can follow you to a new phone. You can delete your account, and everything we hold with it, from inside the App at any time. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

1. Information on your device

Your learning happens on your phone. If you do not sign in, none of it is ever sent to us. This includes:

  • Your study progress and spaced-repetition schedule (which words are due, how well you know them).
  • Your card deck, streak, and daily activity.
  • Your settings (theme, audio speed, and similar preferences).
  • Audio clips the App has downloaded or generated so lessons play offline.
  • If you use "Record me" (in the "Listen, pause, say" practice or the Speak task of "Practise the four skills"), your most recent recording, so you can play it back. Only the last one is kept, it is deleted when you leave that screen, and it is never uploaded.
  • What you type in the Write task of "Practise the four skills", so your draft is still there when you come back. It is never uploaded.

If you sign in, a copy of some of this progress is also backed up to our servers, as described in section 2. Your recordings, drafts and settings never are.

If you uninstall the App, this on-device information is removed with it.

2. Information we handle only if you sign in (optional)

Signing in is optional. It is needed only for the online leaderboard, the cloud backup of your progress, and sentence mining. If you choose to sign in with Google, we handle:

  • Your Google account email and a user ID. Used to create and secure your account. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • A display name and city. Your display name defaults to your city, which is guessed from your device's time-zone setting (not your GPS location); you can change it any time. Your display name, city, and total days practised are shown to other learners on the public leaderboard.
  • Your total days practised. Used to rank you on the leaderboard.
  • A backup of your learning progress. So your climb can be restored on a new phone or after a reinstall, we keep: the review schedule of the words you have studied (when each is next due and how well you know it), the days you studied, your place on the climb (current stop, checkpoints passed, your free level-check result), your daily new-word pace, and a few markers such as whether you have seen the welcome screen or used your free sentence mine. This copy is private: only you, signed in, can read it. It contains no recordings, no drafts, and no word lists beyond the progress numbers.

3. Sentence mining

Sentence mining needs you to be signed in. Everyone gets one free mine, and Premium members can mine more. When you use the "Mine a sentence" feature, the French sentence you submit is sent to Google's services (Google Gemini for translation and Google Cloud Text-to-Speech for audio) so the App can build a flashcard from it. We do not store the text of your sentence on our servers; we keep only a daily count of how many sentences you have mined, to apply fair-use limits. The generated audio is cached on your phone so the card plays offline afterwards.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to manage your Premium status across your devices; RevenueCat processes your purchase history and an app-specific identifier for this purpose. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

5. Permissions the App uses

  • Internet. Only for the optional leaderboard, the cloud backup, sentence mining, and purchases. Core learning works without it.
  • Notifications. Used only for a single on-device reminder shortly before a free trial would start billing, so you are not surprised. You can turn this off in your phone settings.
  • Microphone. Used only when you tap "Record me" (in the "Listen, pause, say" practice or the Speak task of "Practise the four skills"), to record you speaking French so you can listen back. The App asks the first time you tap it, never before. The recording stays on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can turn this off in your phone settings; everything else keeps working.

The App does not request your precise location, contacts, photos, or camera.

6. Third parties we rely on

  • Supabase (authentication, the leaderboard, and the cloud backup of your progress)
  • Google (Play Billing, and, for the mining feature only, Gemini and Cloud Text-to-Speech)
  • RevenueCat (managing your Premium status)

We do not use advertising networks, and we do not use analytics or tracking software.

7. Data security and retention

Information sent to our servers is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Account, leaderboard and backup information is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, all of it is removed from our systems at once.

8. Deleting your account and data

From inside the App: open Settings, and under Account tap Delete my account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account, your leaderboard entry, your cloud backup and your sentence-mining count, straight away. The learning on your phone stays until you clear it (Settings, Start over) or uninstall the App.

Without the App (for example, if you have already uninstalled it): email us from the Google account you signed in with, and we will delete it for you. The steps are also on our account deletion page.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription; cancel that in Google Play. You can also simply sign out at any time in Settings. Uninstalling the App removes all on-device data.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
